Game Dev Rotation

The courses that matter most for building 3D, multiplayer, systems-heavy games: Godot, Blender, UI and game design. Every week rotates through several tracks so nothing goes cold, and beginner courses come before intermediate ones within each track.

Rotation map

one column per week · brighter = more hours

Each row is a track. Godot and Blender show up almost every week; smaller tracks come back every week or two. A few courses wait for a prerequisite, such as the Godot UI course, which starts once you are about 60% through Godot 3D.

Weekly plan

The schedule stays fixed. Each block fills in as you log hours, and shows hours done out of hours planned. Work ahead and later blocks fill in on their own. If you fall behind, press Re-plan from this week to rebuild the remaining work from today. Start each block with a 10-minute redo of the last lesson in that track.

Courses and progress

log hours as you go

Type the hours you just worked into a course's Log box and press Log, or drag its slider for a rough set. Hours count as planned hours, so 3 hours logged is 3 hours done. Items tagged Added are free or paid resources I found to fill gaps in your list.

After the main plan

tracked, scheduled once the main plan ends

These courses add polish rather than anything required to build a game, so they are not part of the main schedule or its dates. They get their own weeks after the main plan ends, one at a time. You can log hours on them the same way.
